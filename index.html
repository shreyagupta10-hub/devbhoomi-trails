Five places worth the journey

From lakes to river towns, here is where to go and what to do there.

Nainital

Boating on the lake, Mall Road walks, Snow View point

Rishikesh

River rafting, Ganga aarti, yoga

Mussoorie

Kempty Falls, cable car, cafes

Auli

Skiing and Himalayan views

Jim Corbett

Jungle safari and wildlife

When to go

Pick your month before you pick your bags.

Plan your trip in four steps

Follow these in order and you will not miss anything.

1. Choose the place and month

Use the table above to match your interest with the season.

2. Book travel and stay

Book trains and hotels early, especially for weekends and holidays.

3. Pack for the weather

Carry warm layers, comfortable shoes, a power bank and your ID.

4. Travel responsibly

Carry your waste back and respect local people and wildlife.
